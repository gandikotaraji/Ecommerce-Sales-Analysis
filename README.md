# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify business trends, evaluate sales performance, and generate meaningful business insights. The analysis explores revenue, profitability, product categories, customer segments, regional performance, and sales trends to support data-driven business decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Understand customer segment purchasing patterns.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Identify opportunities to improve business profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning and Analysis
- Charts and Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis
- Business Intelligence

## 📂 Dataset Information
- **Total Records:** 9,994
- **Total Columns:** 22
- **Time Period:** 2011–2014
- **Key Attributes:** Order ID, Order Date, Customer ID, Segment, Region, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|

| Total Profit | $286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Total Customers | 793 |

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed
1. **Sales Performance Analysis:** Evaluated overall revenue and profit trends.
2. **Category Analysis:** Compared sales and profitability across product categories.
3. **Sub-Category Analysis:** Identified high-performing product sub-categories.
4. **Regional Analysis:** Examined sales contribution across different regions.
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzed sales and profit across customer segments.
6. **Time-Based Analysis:** Studied monthly and yearly sales patterns.

## 💡 Business Insights
- Identified the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Evaluated yearly sales and profit growth.
- Identified monthly sales patterns to support business planning.
- Compared regional and customer-segment contributions.
- Highlighted opportunities for improving product profitability and discount strategies.
